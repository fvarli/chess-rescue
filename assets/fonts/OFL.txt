In this repository:
  Inter-Regular.ttf, Inter-Medium.ttf, Inter-SemiBold.ttf, Inter-Bold.ttf
  are bundled and declared in pubspec.yaml as the family `Inter`. The app
  loads them via Flutter's standard font-asset pipeline; the in-app theme
  (`lib/core/theme/app_theme.dart`) references `fontFamily: 'Inter'` for
  display copy and falls back to the platform monospace for micro-labels.
