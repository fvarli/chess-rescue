# Archived — FI1 (forcedInterposition / "martyr")

## Original memo summary

**Motif.** `forcedInterposition` (the everyday chess word: "martyr").

**Proposed concept.** Sketched a sacrifice-interposition shape similar to B1's `Rd4` but with a different rescuer piece (knight instead of rook), aiming to feel like a distinct martyr exercise.

## Rejection record

- **Decision.** NOT PURSUED.
- **Phase.** Phase 1 candidate generation.
- **Reviewer.** Author self-rejection during memo writing.
- **Reason.** The author could not find a `forcedInterposition` shape that:
  1. Reads instantly (per the lead's *"only if reads instantly"* qualifier for this priority-5 motif)
  2. Is geometrically distinct from B1's `Rd4` rescue
  3. Doesn't slide into either `blockFile` / `sealDiagonal` (which are about INTERPOSITION without the sacrifice element) or `removeDefender` (capturing rather than interposing)

  Every shape considered either re-skinned B1 or felt mechanically muddled. The candidate was deferred without committing to a specific position.
- **Future-sprint guidance.** The `forcedInterposition` motif is **narrow by nature**. It requires:
  - An open line (diagonal or file) the bK or bQ commands
  - A white piece that can land in the middle of the line
  - The piece will be captured next move (sacrifice = "martyr") — that captures *itself* defangs the threat by deflecting the attacker

  The narrowness is why B1 is the lone exemplar today. New `forcedInterposition` positions are unlikely to be authored frequently. Acceptable future paths:
  - **Different rescuer piece type** that's worth sacrificing (a bishop instead of B1's rook, or a queen for a more dramatic feel)
  - **Different sacrifice value** (the captured piece's value vs. the threat avoided creates emotional weight)
  - **Doubled threat** (the sacrifice deflects an attack while *also* opening a counter-tactic for white next move — but this risks multi-move framing)

  Expect future sprints to produce 0-1 `forcedInterposition` positions max. The motif's natural ceiling is low.
